# Yopu Chord Format & Notation Reference

## Supported Chords
- **Triads**: Major (`C`, `G`, `F#`), Minor (`Am`, `Em`, `Bbm`)
- **7ths**: Dominant 7 (`G7`, `C7`), Major 7 (`Cmaj7`, `Fmaj7`), Minor 7 (`Am7`, `Em7`), Diminished 7 (`Bdim7`)
- **Extensions**: 9ths (`C9`, `Am9`, `Cadd9`), 11ths, 13ths, 6/9
- **Suspended & Altered**: `sus2`, `sus4`, `dim`, `aug`, `m7b5`, `7b5`, `7#9`
- **Slash Chords**: `G/B`, `C/E`, `D/F#`, `A/C#`, `F/A`
